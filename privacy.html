Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

HostMatrix (hostmatrix.bunon.xyz) is a free directory of cloud hosting platforms. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site and how you can control it.

Information we collect

HostMatrix has no accounts, sign-up forms or backend database. We do not ask for or store your name, email address or other personal details. Your filter choices and wizard answers stay in your browser.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed, which hosting providers are opened and which search terms are entered in the directory. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as your approximate location, device type, browser and pages visited. IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics 4. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Cookie consent

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before analytics or advertising cookies are used. Until you choose, Google tags run in a restricted, cookieless mode. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown by the consent message.

Affiliate links

Some links to hosting providers may be affiliate or referral links, marked as sponsored. If you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This does not change how providers are listed or ranked.

Third-party websites

HostMatrix links to external hosting providers. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies before signing up.

Children's privacy

HostMatrix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.
